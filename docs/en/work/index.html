Freedom
2020-PRESENT · Senior Full-Stack Developer

Auditable paper trading

Evidence-first
paper trading

Freedom is a B3 research and paper-trading system I built around reproducible evidence. Its active momentum strategy uses completed daily data, risk controls, and a persistent simulated broker; experimental ML has no trade authority.

CHALLENGE
Turn B3 market data and trading research into reproducible paper decisions without look-ahead leakage, invented fills, or unsupported performance claims.
ROLE
Senior Full-Stack Developer
APPROACH
  • Built canonical market-data ingestion and immutable snapshots with point-in-time identity, correction history, and reproducible feature inputs.
  • Implemented a deterministic B3 momentum strategy with completed-D1 setup confirmation and backtests that separate signal quality from trade outcomes.
  • Connected approved decisions to paper-only risk controls, a persistent simulated broker, reconciliation, and idempotent replay after interruptions.
  • Kept experimental ML behind qualification gates; the current unqualified models and sealed holdout cannot drive the paper strategy.
IMPACT
Delivered an operator-runnable paper-trading path whose decisions, simulated fills, and outcomes can be traced and replayed, without claiming proven profitability or real-money execution.
LESSON
In financial systems, reproducibility, failure recovery, and an honest 'not qualified' result are product features, not obstacles to shipping.
